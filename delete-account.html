Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 19, 2026

You can delete your 10Life account and all of its data at any time, directly from the app. Deletion is permanent and immediate. This page explains how, and what gets removed.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open 10Life.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Data & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Once you confirm, your account and all associated data are permanently deleted.

What gets deleted

All of your information is removed: your account and profile, financial entries (transactions, budgets, goals, accounts and net worth), and productivity entries (tasks, habits, and projects), along with any other content you created in the app.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We do not retain your data after deletion, except for anything that may remain temporarily in routine encrypted backups before they rotate and are overwritten.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to delete your account from within the app, email us from your registered address at sergio.posada@alanthic.co with the subject "Delete account", and we'll process the deletion for you.
