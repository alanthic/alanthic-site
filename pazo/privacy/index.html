Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Pazo Run ("Pazo") is published by Alanthic LLC, Houston, Texas. This policy explains what Pazo stores, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@alanthic.co.

What Pazo stores

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Your name and emailYour Google or Apple sign-inTo identify your account
Settings: units, first day of the week, birth year and max heart rate (both optional)You, in the appTo build your plan and your heart-rate guide
Your plan answers (goal, race date, how you run today, your answers to the health questions) and the plan itselfYou, in the appTo build and show your training plan
Your runs: date, start and end time, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate, elevation gain, and speed and heart-rate samples during the runHealth Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), only with your permission; or typed in by youTo mark workouts as done and show your progress
Your watch pairing: watch model, app version and a secret only your watch knows (we keep only its hash)Your Garmin watch, when you pair itTo send your workouts to the watch
Your subscription status and an anonymous purchase historyApple or Google, through RevenueCatTo know whether your subscription is active

Pazo reads Health Connect and Apple Health; it never writes to them. Pazo does not collect your location or the GPS route of your runs, your contacts, or anything from other apps. There is no advertising in Pazo and no ad tracking.

Heart rate, your runs and your health answers are health data. We use them only to run the app for you. We never sell them and never use them for advertising. Washington residents: see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and servers, United States) stores the data above on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions. It receives your Pazo account ID and your purchases from Apple or Google, never your health data.
  • Apple and Google handle payment. We never see your card details.
  • Sentry receives technical crash reports, stripped of your health data, location and personal details.
  • Garmin: your workouts reach your watch through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, under Garmin's own terms. A workout file can include its pace targets and your heart-rate guide (for example "HR < 145").

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your data is limited to your own account.

Your partner

If you link your account with another Pazo user (Settings → Partner), and only after you both agree, each of you sees the other's first name, plan goal and race date, and the current week: planned workouts, whether each was done or skipped, and each run's date, time, duration and distance. Your partner never sees your heart rate, heart-rate samples or health answers. You can end the link at any time, and sharing stops at once.

Reminders

Workout reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Your choices

  • Turn off Health Connect or Apple Health access at any time in your phone's settings. Pazo then stops reading new runs.
  • Unpair your watch in Settings → Watch, or end a partner link in Settings → Partner.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for a correction, at support@alanthic.co.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Then everything above is deleted at once. Records Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep about your purchases follow their own retention rules.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Or see how to delete your account without the app. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Children

Pazo is not meant for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
