Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alanthic LLC ("Alanthic", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the 10Life mobile application ("the App"). By using 10Life you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

10Life is a personal finance and productivity app — it helps you track expenses, budgets and goals, organize tasks and habits, and capture entries by voice. It is published by Alanthic LLC, Houston, Texas, United States. You can reach us at sergio.posada@alanthic.co.

Information we collect

Account information

When you first open 10Life we create an anonymous account so you can use the App without signing up. If you choose to secure your account, you may link an email and password, or sign in with Google or Apple, in which case we receive your name and email address from that provider. You may also optionally add a display name, handle, avatar, short bio, country, preferred currency, and timezone.

Content you create

The App stores the information you enter so it can provide the service and sync it across your devices — for example financial entries (transactions, budgets, goals, accounts and net worth) and productivity entries (tasks, habits, and projects). This content belongs to you.

Voice capture

When you use voice capture, speech is transcribed on your device by your operating system's speech recognizer — your audio never leaves your phone and we never receive or store it. The resulting text transcript is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for the sole purpose of interpreting it into a draft entry (for example, turning "spent 12 on coffee" into an expense). Transcripts are not used to train AI models.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can deliver the reminders and updates you've turned on. You can disable notifications at any time in the App or your device settings.

Diagnostics

To keep the App stable, we collect crash reports and error diagnostics (via Sentry). These help us find and fix bugs and are not used to identify you.

Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, billing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status (active, trial, expired) but not your full payment card details.

How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and sync the App's features.
  • To interpret your voice transcripts into draft entries.
  • To send the notifications you have enabled.
  • To manage subscriptions, trials, and access to premium features.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third‑party advertising.

Service providers

We share information only with the service providers that make the App work, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account and content.
  • Google and Apple — sign‑in, when you choose to use them.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.
  • Anthropic — interpreting voice transcripts into entries.
  • Expo and the platform push services (Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivering notifications.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App (Profile → Account), or by emailing us at sergio.posada@alanthic.co. You can also export a copy of your data from within the App.

Security

We protect your data with row‑level access controls and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information and limit access to it.

Children

10Life is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

Alanthic LLC is based in the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App you consent to this transfer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can exercise most of these directly in the App, or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice within the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email sergio.posada@alanthic.co.
